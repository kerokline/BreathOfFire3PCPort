**Breath of Fire III - bof3x v0.1.1-beta.2** - a player release: the second
beta with the fixes from play since 2026-11-24. The music no longer starts a
new song with a burst of the last one, the Config screen at New Game has its
own sounds, and four scenes are drawn correctly. The list is under "Fixed
and changed since v0.1.0-beta.2".

bof3x is a renovation of the 2001 Chinese PC port of *Breath of Fire III*:
every function of the game's own code is our C++, our `bof3x.exe` runs it
with no code of Capcom's mapped, and the game can be installed from the PC
port, from a PlayStation disc alone, or from a PSP disc alone. **This
download holds no game data.** You add your own copy, and everything the game
reads is built on your machine from it.

## Getting started (Windows)

1. Unzip into a folder with a **short path**, e.g. `C:\Games\bof3x`.
2. Run `bof3x-launcher.exe`. The first time, it opens on the **Sources**
   page; later, it is under **Configure**, the **Data** tab.
3. On the **Sources** page, add what you own:
   - the PC port's game folder (the one holding `BOF3.exe` and `DAT`), the
     2001-04-18 Chinese release, unmodified;
   - a PlayStation disc as `.cue`/`.bin` - US, Japanese, European (English),
     French or German;
   - a PSP disc as `.iso` - Japanese or European;
   - or a `.zip` holding any of these;
   - or a whole folder of them: **Folder...** checks every image in it (and
     the folders below it) and adds each one that matches a known release.

   Each one is checked against the known releases by its files' hashes. The
   first source makes the install; each later one adds what it carries, and
   the install is rebuilt. Expect a few minutes per setup.
4. Play.

Everything is written inside the folder you unzipped. Your own files are
only read.

**Upgrading from v0.1.0-beta.2, beta.1 or an alpha:** unzip over the old
folder and start the launcher. It checks your install against the release.
When a piece of the install would be built differently, it names the pieces
and offers to rebuild them (**No** starts nothing and leaves the install as
it is). The rebuild keeps the parts that did not change, and the saves and
the settings are kept. The upgrade from v0.1.0-beta.2 was not run on a
released zip; an install with the PSP's graphics gets the rebuild offer,
because the demo's badge is a new piece.

**Your saves.** The game's own save files are kept through every upgrade and
load as before. **A quicksave from v0.1.0-beta.2 or older is refused by this
release:** the game says so on screen and does not touch the file. Before
you upgrade, make a game save. From this release on, some quicksaves load
in another build (below).

## Getting started (Linux, new)

`bof3x-v0.1.1-beta.2-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` is a native build for x86-64
Linux. **It is new, and only its first scenes have been played.** One
person played the first scenes on one laptop (GNOME on Wayland): the
picture, the sound and the controls were right, and the story went on. On
ten scripted routes it computes the same game as the Windows builds, tick
for tick; one shop jingle's timing is the known exception. Installs from the
PC folder, from a US PlayStation disc alone and from a European PSP disc
alone, and the game's self-tests passed on a computer with no screen.
Nobody has played it further, or on another desktop or graphics card. If
you try it, please tell us what you see.

You need:

- a 64-bit Linux system with glibc 2.38 or newer (Ubuntu 24.04 or newer, or
  a system of the same age). The tarball's `README.txt` gives the exact
  versions;
- a Vulkan 1.3 graphics driver, and X11 or Wayland;
- Python 3.11 or newer with Pillow and capstone. The setup uses them. The
  tarball holds no Python;
- ffmpeg, optional, as on Windows.

To start:

1. Extract the tarball to a folder you can write to.
2. In that folder: `python3 -m venv runtime/python`, then
   `runtime/python/bin/pip install -r runtime/tools/requirements.txt`. On
   Debian and Ubuntu, `python3 -m venv` needs the package `python3-venv`.
   If there is no `runtime/python`, the launcher uses `python3` from your
   `PATH`.
3. Start `./bof3x-launcher` and add your copy of the game on the Sources
   page.

To check that your download is the file this project built (GitHub's CLI):

```
gh attestation verify bof3x-v0.1.1-beta.2-linux-x86_64.tar.gz --repo kerokline/BreathOfFire3PCPort
```

The Windows build also runs under Proton and Wine, as before.

## Fixed and changed since v0.1.0-beta.2

- **Music: a song change no longer starts with a burst of the last song.**
  With the PlayStation's or the PSP's sequenced music, the last notes and
  the echo of a stopped song sounded over the start of the next song - a
  short "ping" at an area change. Now an area's new song starts on silence.
  At the start of a battle and at the fanfares, the last notes of the old
  song end under the new song, through their own instruments. The area
  changes were heard in play; the battle's start was heard as a render only.
- **New Game: the Config screen has its own sounds.** With the PlayStation's
  New Game screens, the Config screen played other sound effects in place
  of its beep. Heard in play.
- **Scenes at a wide picture:**
  - on the Yraall road, Teepo is no longer drawn over Ryu through his kick;
  - at Bunyan's table, the table covers Rei's tail, the tail is drawn over
    the table while he swings it at the plate, and the plate's edges are
    not cut;
  - the sunset's sky is orange at 16:9 and 21:9, as at 4:3.

  These were measured on scripted routes, not seen in play.
- **The quicksave menu (F5) has its backdrop in a battle.** Before, it was
  drawn on black there. Not played by a person.
- **The PSP's graphics draw the PSP's demo badge.** In the attract demo,
  the corner badge had the PSP's colours on the PlayStation's picture. With
  the PSP's graphics on, the badge is now the PSP's own, whole. With a
  Japanese and a European PSP disc, the European badge is used.
- **A save keeps the place the party came from.** A save made in an
  encounter field or at the fishing spot now leaves to the right place
  after a load. A save from before this release, loaded at the fishing
  spot: the fishing menu's leave choice does nothing, where the game
  stopped before.
- **Some quicksaves load in another build** (new, not tried by a person). A
  quicksave made at one of fifteen points of the game loads in a later
  build, in the 32-bit and the 64-bit build, and on Linux. Any other
  quicksave is refused by another build, and the game offers the save that
  the play started from.

## ffmpeg (optional, recommended)

The PSP's sound effects, voices and logo movie, and the repair of the PC
port's logo movie (Indeo 5, which current Windows cannot play) are
converted by **your** ffmpeg. Get a build from
<https://ffmpeg.org/download.html>, then put it on your `PATH` or locate it
in the launcher, and run the setup again. Without it those pieces are
skipped and the setup says which.

## What works

- Installs from the PC port, from any one catalogued PlayStation disc alone,
  from a PSP disc alone, or a mix; English, French, German and Japanese from
  the matching disc, and any two of them together as a row above the text.
- A full play to the ending and the clear save, by hand (beta.1's two
  plays).
- The PlayStation's sequenced music from your disc, the PSP's, or the PC's
  MP3s; the Jukebox in the launcher.
- Quicksaves alongside the game's own saves, in the field and in battle.
- Presets, and single changes turned off by name.
- Direct3D 11, windowed or borderless fullscreen, integer scaling, widescreen
  (16:10, 16:9 and 21:9), the point filter and CRT looks, modern pads
  through SDL3, sound through SDL3.
- Signed Windows executables (Authenticode, timestamped); a build
  attestation for the Linux tarball.
- The fixes and options listed in the project's README, each recorded in
  `docs/DIVERGENCE.md`.

## Known issues

- **Issue #190, a crash at the dragon gene window in battle.** It was not
  reproduced, and the issue is closed. **If it happens to you,** add a
  comment to issue #190: the
  `CRASH n:` lines from `bof3x.log` in your folder, what the Sources page
  lists, and your language. **Do not attach the `bof3x.crash-*.dmp`
  files**: they hold the game's data.
- **A mod added before v0.1.0-beta.2** from the Definitive Edition's patch:
  remove it and add the patch again, so that it gets the new behaviours.
  The same holds for *Better Descriptions* added before 2026-11-15.
- **Furigana does not build from this release** (SudachiPy is not bundled),
  and its readings are not proofread.
- At 21:9 a menu box that slides in can be seen whole in the side band on
  its first frames.
- Some labels and text painted into artwork stay Chinese on a PC-based
  install.
- **Not read yet:** `.chd` and `.cso` images (convert them back to
  `.cue`/`.bin` or `.iso`), and 2048-byte `.iso` images of a PlayStation
  disc - use the `.bin`/`.cue`.
- **Linux:** a PSP install made without ffmpeg has no effects, voices or
  logo movie, as on Windows.
